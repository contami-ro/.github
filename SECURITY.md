# Security Policy

Contami processes invoicing, banking, and tax data for Romanian PFA holders.
Treat anything touching ANAF/SPV credentials, bank statements, user account
data, or the multi-tenant boundary as security-sensitive.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **razvan@timis.dev** directly. Do not open a GitHub issue for a security
report, even for something that looks minor — what seems harmless in one repo
can chain into something worse across them.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and how to reach you. You will
get a reply quickly, usually within a few days.
